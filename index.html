Mandatory Safety Inspection (Every 5 Years)

As per the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas guidelines, a mandatory safety check of your domestic LPG installation must be conducted by an authorized mechanic once every 5 years. Regular inspection prevents gas leaks, detects worn-out equipment, and ensures the safety of your family and home.

What Gets Inspected?

  • Suraksha LPG Hose: Checking for cracks, wear, or expiry.
  • Pressure Regulator: Testing regulator locking mechanism and O-ring seal.
  • LPG Gas Stove & Burners: Checking flame quality and burner efficiency.
  • Leakage Testing: Soap solution leak test across joints and cylinder pad.
  • Installation Positioning: Verification of proper cylinder positioning and ventilation.

Essential LPG Safety Rules

  1. Always insist on a Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) when receiving a cylinder refill.
  2. Only allow authorized Bharatgas mechanics carrying valid ID cards to inspect your installation.
  3. Always use a certified Suraksha Hose and replace it every 5 years.
  4. Switch off the pressure regulator knob when the stove is not in use.
  5. If you smell gas: Turn off the regulator, open windows, and do NOT operate electrical switches.
